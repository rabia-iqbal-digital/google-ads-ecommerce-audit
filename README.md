## Project Overview

This project is a structured Google Ads performance audit for an e-commerce beauty brand operating in the Pakistani market.

The audit examines the available campaign-level information across **Search and Demand Generation campaigns** and evaluates performance from a performance-marketing perspective.

The objective was to identify:

- Performance inefficiencies
- Campaign and targeting issues
- Opportunities for budget optimization
- Search-term and keyword opportunities
- Audience and geographic inefficiencies
- Ad asset and account QA issues
- Landing-page and conversion considerations
- Prioritized optimization actions

The project demonstrates how I approach a Google Ads account from an **analysis → diagnosis → prioritization → optimization** perspective.

## Key Findings

### 01 | Campaign efficiency varied significantly

The available performance data showed meaningful differences in efficiency between campaign types and periods.

This created an opportunity to review budget allocation based on validated performance rather than treating all campaigns equally.

---

### 02 | Search showed stronger observed efficiency

The Search campaign demonstrated substantially stronger observed conversion efficiency than the Demand Generation campaign in the reviewed data.

This suggests that higher-intent traffic should receive careful consideration when evaluating budget allocation and campaign priorities.

---

### 03 | Audience performance required tighter optimization

The available audience-level data indicated meaningful differences in observed cost efficiency.

The audit therefore recommends reducing or pausing inefficient audience segments while protecting and testing segments demonstrating stronger performance.

---

### 04 | Geographic performance presented optimization opportunities

The available location-level data indicated variation in observed performance across cities.

The recommended approach is to identify consistently inefficient locations, reduce wasted spend where supported by sufficient data, and prioritize locations demonstrating stronger conversion efficiency.

---

### 05 | Search-term management can improve traffic quality

The search-term review identified opportunities for:

- Additional negative keywords
- Relevant search-term expansion
- Improved query control
- Better alignment between search intent and landing pages

---

### 06 | Ad asset QA issues require attention

The audit identified asset-related issues, including business logo and asset eligibility concerns.

These should be reviewed, corrected, replaced, or appealed where appropriate to protect campaign eligibility and ad quality.

---

### 07 | Landing-page conversion opportunities exist

The landing-page review identified opportunities around:

- CTA prominence
- Mobile presentation
- Offer communication
- Product credibility
- Urgency messaging
- Promotional communication
- Conversion-focused page hierarchy

These recommendations are presented as optimization opportunities rather than measured conversion improvements.
